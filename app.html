RomanYear

Turn a number or year into Roman numerals, or read Roman numerals as a number.

About this

Standard form uses subtractive pairs IV, IX, XL, XC, CD, CM, and tops out at 3999 (MMMCMXCIX), as described on Wikipedia. Clock faces often write 4 as IIII, and the Colosseum gate 44 is XLIIII. The reader accepts the standard form and the clock style, and tells you the standard form when a numeral is non-standard (IC is not 99). Runs in your browser.
